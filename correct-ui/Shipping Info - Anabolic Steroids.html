Shipping Info

Reading Time: 2 min

HOW DO WE SHIP?

All products are shipped World Wide and the packages are very discreet.

The name of our Organization or something that would imply pharmaceutical contents is never used in our shipments.

The size of the packages is not large, that thing reduces unnecessary suspicion. If the order is too large to fit in one package, it will automatically be divided into two or more packages.

The packages are sent from different European countries.

HOW DO WE SHIP BULK/LARGE ORDERS?


Usually the order is divided into necessary amount of packages. We ensure you the same discretion level as used for small sized orders.

SHIPPING COST?


The shipping cost is 50 USD.

CAN I CHANGE MY ORDER AFTER IT HAS BEEN PLACED?


If your order is unpaid you can edit it by logging into your account and choose order ID and click on EDIT ORDER.

If your order is paid but it was not sent yet and you want to add or to remove an item from the order, you should create a ticket and ask Customer Support to make needed changes. But please remember to specify order ID in the ticket.

HOW SUCCESSFUL IS DELIVERY?


We ensure you that all packages are shipped with maximum discretion. All the products are packed with extreme care and precaution.
